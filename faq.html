Products and prices

What are you selling, exactly?

Downloadable n8n workflow packs. You buy a ZIP, import the workflow files into your own n8n, and the workflows run on your machine or server. There are two packs, a bundle of both, and six individual workflows.

What does the Small-Biz Lead-Gen Pack do? ($39)

It runs a three-step lead pipeline. (1) Lead Finder: you give it a business type and a location, and it pulls real businesses from OpenStreetMap into a list with name, address, phone, website, email, and coordinates. (2) AI Enrichment: it builds a short AI snapshot of each lead — what they do, likely pain points, and a personalization hook for your pitch. (3) Outreach Drafts: it writes one personalized draft email per lead. Every draft is locked to human review — nothing sends until you approve it.

What does the Client Onboarding Pack do? ($39)

It handles new-client intake in three steps. (1) Intake Capture: a shareable "Start a project" form validates each submission, adds the client to your client list, and queues a confirmation email draft. (2) AI Proposal Draft: for each new client, AI drafts a personalized proposal email plus a scope checklist and red flags for your eyes only. It never invents prices — there is a [YOUR PRICE] placeholder you fill in. (3) Follow-up Nudges: it finds proposals that have gone quiet past your threshold (default 4 days, adjustable) and drafts one polite follow-up per run.

What's the Full Pipeline Bundle? ($59)

Both packs together — all six workflows. Bought one at a time, the six workflows cost $114. The bundle is $59, so you save $55.

Can I buy just one workflow? ($19 each)

Yes. Each of the six workflows is sold on its own for $19: Lead Finder, AI Enrichment, Outreach Drafts, Intake Capture, AI Proposal Draft, and Follow-up Nudges. Bought singly, the three in a pack cost $57 versus $39 for the pack.

Why these prices?

What's inside / how it works

What do I actually get when I buy?

An instant ZIP download from Payhip. Each pack contains the workflow files (JSON) plus a step-by-step setup README with a $0 setup guide, troubleshooting, and pricing/positioning notes. The Onboarding Pack also includes two starter CSV files (clients and outbox) that its workflows use.

How do I install a workflow?

In n8n: Workflows → Import from File, and pick the JSON file. The setup README walks through the rest step by step.

What is n8n? Do I have to pay for it?

n8n is the automation platform these workflows run on. The Community Edition is free to self-host, and that is what these packs are built for. There is no software bill in the stack we ship.

Do I need API keys?

Lead Finder needs none — no API key, no signup; it reads free OpenStreetMap data through the Overpass API. The AI steps (Enrichment, Outreach Drafts, Proposal Draft, Follow-up Nudges) run on OpenRouter's free-tier models, which need no credit card. The setup README covers the free setup.

Where does the lead data come from?

OpenStreetMap, queried through its Overpass API — free map data. Lead Finder outputs business name, address, phone, website, email, and coordinates where OpenStreetMap has them. Coverage depends on what OpenStreetMap has mapped in the area you search.

What kinds of businesses can I search for?

The packs are built for local businesses — the listings name dentists, plumbers, salons, law firms, and restaurants as examples. Niches and search radius are customizable in plainly labeled nodes.

Can I customize the workflows?

Yes — that is the point of owning the files. Niches, radius, prompts, form fields, the follow-up timing, proposal voice, and models all live in plainly labeled nodes you can change.

The human-approval rule

Does it send emails automatically?

No. Never. AI drafts. You approve. Nothing sends automatically — ever. Every outreach email, confirmation, proposal, and follow-up is produced as a draft locked to "DRAFT — HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED" until you read it and send it yourself.

Why don't the workflows just send?

Because a message with your name on it should have your eyes on it first. The human review gate is a feature, not a limitation.
